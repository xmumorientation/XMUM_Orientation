3.1 Day 1 Games: Play and Give

When the game is over, choose each group's result and give the tokens.

  1. 1
    Win for the winning group. The amount shown is the reward.
  2. 2
    Participation for a group that took part but did not win.
  3. 3
    Tap Submit Result (or Submit Results for two groups). It stays grey until every group has a result.
  4. 4
    Cancel Game ends the game without giving any tokens.
  1. 1
    Read each group's result, the tokens added and the new balance.
  2. 2
    Give Tokens sends it. Tap Cancel to go back and change a result.
Important

The amounts (Win and Participation) are set by Admin in the token rules. You cannot choose them. In a head-to-head, both groups can win.

Tip

Check the group names on the sheet before you tap Give Tokens. That is the moment to catch a mistake.

3.1 Day 1 Games: After Giving

Check the message, and know how to undo a mistake.

  1. 1
    The green message lists each group, the tokens added and the new balance, for example Group 1 +5 / 125.

Good to Know

Your station goes back to Available and the groups are checked out, ready for the next game.

  1. 1
    The sheet shows what will be reversed: the group, the reason, the tokens and how long ago.
  2. 2
    Undo takes those tokens back. Cancel keeps everything as it is.
Important

Undo reverses only your last transaction, and only inside the time window Admin set (10 minutes by default). It is for Day 1 rewards. After the window, ask Admin.

3.2 Day 2 Games: Checks and Risk Levels

If a check has a red cross, the game cannot start. Read the line, it tells you why.

  1. 1
    Tokens: Has 0, needs 2. Short by 2. The group cannot pay the entry fee.
  2. 2
    Start Game stays grey. Pick another group or tell this group.
  1. 1
    Pick the locations. Medium Risk: pick 2. High Risk: pick 1. Each card shows how many of its 5 pieces the group has.
Low RiskNo picking. A win gives a random piece from any location.
Medium RiskPick 2. A win gives a random piece from them.
High RiskPick 1. A win gives a piece of that location.
✓

Green tick: fine.

!

Amber note: you may start, but read it (for example a full location).

✕

Red cross: blocked. You cannot start.

·

Grey dot: still checking, or waiting for your pick.

  3. 3
    Cancel goes back so you can pick the other result.
Important

The entry fee is charged on submit, win or lose. Confirm the result before you tap the button.

In ProgressA game is running. Set by Start Game.

3.5 My Activity and Offline Mode

Check what you gave, and know what to do when the signal drops.

  1. 1
    Tap Activity at the top right of the Station Panel. The list shows the token changes you made, newest first, under their date.
  2. 2
    A change you reversed is marked Undone.

If the Signal Drops

You can still submit results. The website keeps them and sends them when the connection comes back.

  1. 1
    A message Offline Queue Active shows how many submissions are waiting.
  2. 2
    Keep the page open until it clears. Do not close the tab.
  3. 3
    If the server refuses one after you reconnect, a red message says queued submission rejected. It was not applied. Redo it by hand if it is still needed, then tap Dismiss.
Important

Do not submit the same result twice because the screen is slow. The website protects against double charges, but wait for the message first.

7.1 Fixing Problems

What You See on ScreenWhy It Happens, and What to Do
The Station Panel title says No Station Yet and the line under it says Ask Admin to assign your station.Admin has not given you a station, so you cannot run games. Tell Admin.
The station marked NOW is for the wrong day.The panel follows the game phases Admin starts in Live control, not the calendar. When Admin starts the next day's phase, NOW moves by itself. If it has not moved, ask Admin whether that phase has started.
(Day 2) The Start Game button is grey and does nothing.A check above the button has a red cross. Read that line: it says exactly what is wrong. When every check is green or amber, the button turns on.
A check says Tokens: Has 0, needs 2. Short by 2.The group cannot pay the entry fee. Do not start the game. Tell the group, or pick another group.
A check says Day 2 Game: Not started yet.Admin has not started Day 2 in Live control. Wait. The check turns green by itself, with no reload.
A check says Station Game: Switched off by Admin.Admin has paused station games for now. Wait until Admin switches them back on.
A check says Token Freeze.Admin has frozen all tokens for now, so no result can be saved. Wait until Admin unfreezes them.
A check says Station: Closed.Your station is closed, so groups cannot play. Tap Open Station (3.4).
An amber note says Station: Shows In Progress.The website thinks a game is still running at your station. If nobody is playing, you can still start. If an earlier group was left behind, ask HOGM or Admin to clear it.
A check says Puzzle Piece: is full.The group already has all 5 pieces of that location, so a win there gives nothing. On a Medium station the website uses the other location. On a High station, pick a different location.
A red message appears after you tap Submit.The server refused the result and nothing was charged. Read the message, fix the cause (for example not enough tokens), then submit again, or tap Cancel Game.
A message says Offline Queue Active.Your phone has no signal, so your submissions are waiting. Keep this page open. They send by themselves when the signal returns.
A red message says queued submission rejected.A result you submitted offline was refused when the signal came back, so it was not applied. Redo it by hand if it is still needed, then tap Dismiss (3.5).
You gave the wrong result.On Day 1, tap Undo My Last Transaction within the time limit (3.1). After that, or on Day 2, tell Admin straight away.
The game you started is gone after the page reloaded.A running game is saved on the phone that started it. Open the Station Panel again on the same phone and the same browser.
A note says The map could not show the group here.It only means the map could not mark the group at your station. The game and the tokens are fine. Carry on.
Good to Know

Still stuck? Ask your Head of Game Masters (HOGM) or Admin. Tell them what the screen says.

7.2 Dos and Don'ts

Do

  • Check you are on the station marked NOW before the games start.
  • Pick the right group and read the confirm sheet before you submit.
  • Wait for the green message after every submit. It is your proof that the tokens or the piece were given.
  • Keep the Station Panel open on one phone at your station.
  • Charge your phone. Bring a power bank.
  • Tell Admin early if something looks wrong, such as a missing station or a wrong token balance. Small problems are easy to fix early.

Don't

  • Don't tap Start Game for a group that has not arrived.
  • Don't change phones in the middle of a game.
  • Don't tap Submit twice because the screen is slow.
  • Don't promise tokens or pieces outside the website. Only what you submit counts.
  • Don't share your login.
  • Don't forget to Log Out if you used someone else's phone.
