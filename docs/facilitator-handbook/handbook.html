2.4 Edit Your Profile

Tell your group who you are. You can change this any time.

How to Open It

Tap your profile button, then Edit Profile. Or tap the pencil on your card (2.3).

  1. 1
    Add Photo (or Change Photo). Pick a clear photo of your face. The app crops it square and makes it smaller for you.
  2. 2

Tip

Your Name sits above Instagram. Check that it is spelled the way you want Freshies to read it.

3.0 The Checklist

The Checklist is your daily work. Open it with the big round Checklist button in the middle of the tab bar.

  1. 1
    Group Name and Slogan. Set it once.
  2. 2
    Manual Location Update. Tell Admin where your group is.
  3. 3
    Take Attendance. Count the people at your gathering point.
  4. 4
    Live GPS. Shows whether your phone is sending your group's position to the map.

Tap a Task to Open It

Tap the card (or the arrow on the right) to open its form. Only one task is open at a time.

What the Colours Mean

DueAmber. You have not sent it yet, or the last one is more than 30 minutes old. Do it now.
just nowNot amber. Up to date. It shows how long ago you sent it, for example 5 min ago.
✓Tick. A one-time task that is finished (Group Name and Slogan).
Important

Location and attendance are never finished for good. Admin can ask for them at any time, so send a new one about every 30 minutes, and whenever your group moves.

Good to Know

Your partner can send these updates too. The Checklist always shows the latest one sent by anyone in your group.

Count the people with your group and send the numbers. Count Local and International students separately. The total is worked out for you.

  1. 1

  2. 2

3.4 Live GPS

Your phone sends its position so your group appears on the campus map. This is separate from the Manual Location Update.

  1. 1
    Status. Live (green) means your position is being sent.
  2. 2
    Refresh GPS sends your position now. Use it after you move.
  3. 3
    Keep Tracking keeps the screen on so the site keeps sending.
  1. 1
    Try Again after you allow location in your phone settings.
  2. 2
    X closes the message.
  1. 1
    The status says Blocked (red). Allow location (below), then tap Refresh GPS.
How to Allow Location

iPhone (Safari)

Tap aA in the address bar, then Website Settings, Location, Allow. Then tap Try Again.

Android (Chrome)

Tap the icon left of the web address, then Permissions, Location, Allow. Then tap Try Again.

Important

A web page cannot send GPS when the screen is locked or the browser is closed. On one phone in your group, switch on Keep Tracking and keep the site open. One phone is enough. The map uses the best recent position from any Facilitator in your group.

7.1 Fixing Problems

What You See on ScreenWhy It Happens, and What to Do
Home says Group Pending, or No partner assigned yet.Admin has not put you in a group yet, so your Group Code and partner cannot show. Tell your Head of Facilitators or Admin. Nothing on your phone fixes this.
The Checklist says You are not in a group yet, or Save gives an error.The same reason. Without a group you cannot save a name, a location update or attendance. Ask Admin to assign you.
Live GPS says Not Allowed Yet.You have not given the site permission to use your location. Tap Allow Location, then tap Allow when your phone asks.
Live GPS says Blocked (red), or a box says Location Is Blocked.Location was refused earlier, so the site cannot send your position. Allow Location for this site in your phone's settings (steps in 3.4), then tap Try Again or Refresh GPS.
Live GPS says Approximate.Your phone is sending only a rough position, so your pin may sit in the wrong place. Turn on Precise Location, go outside or next to a window, then tap Refresh GPS.
Live GPS says Not Sending.Your phone cannot find its position. Turn Location on in your phone's quick settings, and turn Wi-Fi on (it helps even if you do not connect). Then tap Refresh GPS.
The site opened inside Instagram, WhatsApp or another app, and location fails.Apps like these use a built-in browser that often blocks location. Tap the ⋯ menu, choose Open in browser (Safari or Chrome), and log in again.
Your group is missing from the Map, or its pin is old.The map uses GPS from your group's Facilitator phones. On one phone, switch on Keep Tracking, keep the site open with the screen on, then tap Refresh GPS.
An amber Due label is still showing after you sent an update.Due means the last update is more than 30 minutes old, or none was sent. After you tap Send Update or Save, wait for Saved. under the button. The label then turns blue and says just now. If Saved. never appears, your signal may be weak: check your connection and send it again.
You sent the wrong number or the wrong building.Send a new update. Admin always sees the latest one. For a bigger mistake, ask Admin.
A Freshie says the password or QR does not work.Admin may have given your group a new password. Reload your Home page to see the new one and tell the Freshie. The QR itself never changes.
You were logged out.Sign in again (1.1). Everything you saved, such as your group name, profile and past updates, is still there.
Good to Know

Still stuck? Ask your Head of Facilitators (HOF) or a Committee member. Tell them what the screen says.

7.2 Dos and Don'ts

Do

  • Set your Group Name and Slogan and your Profile before the event.
  • Send a Location Update and Attendance about every 30 minutes, and when your group moves.
  • Keep Live GPS on and the site open on one phone in your group.
  • Charge your phone. Bring a power bank.
  • Wait for Saved. to appear under the button before you leave the form. It is your proof that it was sent.
  • Tell Admin early if something looks wrong, such as a wrong group or a missing partner. Small problems are easy to fix early.

Don't

  • Don't share the 4-digit password with other groups.
  • Don't lock the screen or close the browser if you are the tracking phone. GPS stops.
  • Don't share your login with anyone.
  • Don't wait for the amber Due label. Send your update a few minutes early, so Admin always has a recent one.
  • Don't try to open blind boxes or play the games for Freshies. Facilitators can only look.
  • Don't forget to Log Out if you used someone else's phone.
